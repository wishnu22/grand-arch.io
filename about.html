Grand Architecture was founded in 1994 by Mr. N S Narayanan in Thrissur. Founded on the bedrock principle that every home is a lifetime investment, Mr. Narayanan led the practice with an unwavering demand for structural honesty.

Today, our practice operates under the executive stewardship of Manu NR (CEO) and Midhun NR (Business Head), combining three decades of proven engineering with modern tropical residential aesthetics.
