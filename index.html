Studio Heritage

ESTABLISHED 1994.
30+ YEARS
OF CRAFT.

Grand Architecture was founded in 1994 by Mr. N S Narayanan in Thrissur, Kerala. Founded on uncompromising principles of structural integrity and personal accountability, the studio has spent three decades refining the art of residential building.

Today, our practice continues under second-generation leadership, merging our thirty-year foundation of civil mastery with contemporary architectural discipline.
